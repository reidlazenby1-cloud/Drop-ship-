# Operating plan: close-to-$0 start

**Status:** Phase 0 (setup). Last updated 2026-11-16.

**Niche:** home cleaning / "satisfying before-and-after" demos. The reasoning is in [`research/2026-11-16-product-shortlist.md`](research/2026-11-16-product-shortlist.md).

## Why this order

Opening a store first means risking money on a product nobody has proven. Starting with content as an affiliate:

- costs no inventory and no store fees
- builds the audience we'd need later anyway
- shows *which* product people actually buy before we commit to supply.

## What's actually free and what isn't

| Item | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok / Instagram / YouTube Shorts accounts | $0 | Accounts must be yours. |
| Amazon Associates links | $0 | No follower minimum; you must make qualifying sales within the first 180 days to stay in. |
| TikTok Shop Affiliate | $0 | Needs 18+, US, about 1,000 followers. Sellers often send free samples to affiliates. |
| Products to film | **~$15–60** | Not avoidable unless you film things you already own. One spin scrubber is enough to start. |
| CJ Dropshipping account | $0 | No monthly fee or MOQ. You pay per order when it ships. |
| Store (later) | $1/mo for 3 months (Shopify), then ~$29–39/mo; or Whop at $0/mo + ~2.7% + $0.30 per sale | Whop is built mainly for digital goods. Shopify fits physical goods better. |
| Domain (optional) | $0 on a platform subdomain, ~$10–20/yr custom | |
| Ads | $0 | Organic only until a product proves out. |

**Cash-flow warning:** with dropshipping, you pay the supplier when the order ships, but the platform can hold your payout for days. Keep enough on a card to cover the first few orders.

## Phases

### Phase 0: setup (this week)
- [ ] **You:** confirm you're 18+ and US-based, and decide whether you'll appear on camera (hands-only is fine).
- [ ] **You:** create one TikTok account for the niche (also post to Reels and Shorts).
- [ ] **You:** apply to Amazon Associates.
- [ ] **You:** buy one demo product (a spin scrubber in the $15–25 range).
- [ ] **Claude:** write the first 10 video scripts and hooks (viral-hook-formats, ai-ugc-factory).
- [ ] **Claude:** write the bio, link-in-bio page copy and the product-link list.

### Phase 1: content engine (weeks 1–6)
- 1–2 videos per day. Same format, different dirty surfaces.
- Track per video: views, saves, "where do I get this" comments, link clicks, affiliate sales.
- At 1,000 followers: apply to TikTok Shop Affiliate and request free samples (pressure washer gun, pet hair roller).
- **Claude:** reviews the numbers weekly and picks the next products and hooks.

### Phase 2: own-product test (when a product shows repeat purchase intent)
- Get a real supplier quote (CJ, US warehouse first) for the winning product (sourcing-and-fulfillment).
- Order a sample of *our* SKU before selling it.
- Build the store with a differentiated offer (bundle or kit) on Shopify ($1/mo trial) (clone-any-store / increase-average-order-value).
- Sell through our own videos plus TikTok Shop as the seller.

### Phase 3: operate and scale
- Support macros, abandoned-cart flows, chargeback handling (support-automation, abandoned-cart-recovery, chargeback-fighter).
- Paid ads only once organic shows contribution is positive (ad-campaign-scaling).

## Rules we hold ourselves to
- No product launches without a written supplier quote and our own sample.
- No health, safety or "certified" claims that the exact SKU can't back up.
- No kids' toys without a valid Children's Product Certificate (CPC) from the supplier.
- No branded or character knockoffs.
- Anything that spends money, publishes, or contacts a supplier or customer needs your OK.
