# Product shortlist: first pass (2026-11-16)

**Setup we're judging for:** a beginner starting from zero, aiming for close to $0 upfront. That means organic short-form video instead of paid ads, no monthly-fee tools where possible, and dropship suppliers with no minimum order. US buyers.

**Method:** public web search only. The session network blocked CJ Dropshipping and AliExpress catalog pages behind a bot check, so prices come from search-indexed listing pages and retail trackers, not from live catalog reads. Every price below is a **listing snapshot**, not a supplier quote. Unknown costs are marked as such. Planning assumptions are labelled.

---

## The headline finding

**At current prices, none of the commodity viral gadgets clears a safe margin as a stand-alone store.**

Two things changed the math:

1. **The US de minimis exemption is gone for China-origin parcels.** It ended for China/HK on 2025-05-02 and has stayed suspended through 2026. Small parcels now pay full duty. Sources disagree on the exact stack (normal rate + Section 301 + other surcharges), so I used a 25–40% planning range on product value. ([Peregrine](https://peregrineship.com/blog/section-321-de-minimis-2026/), [under30ceo](https://www.under30ceo.com/de-minimis-exemption-small-importers-2026/), [Lume Asia](https://www.lumeasiasourcing.com/insights/the-real-cost-of-importing-from-china-in-2026-now-that-de-minimis-is-gone-lume-asia-sourcing))
2. **Amazon and Walmart sell the same generic items cheaply with fast delivery.** Spin scrubbers routinely sell for $15–$30 there. Generic reusable pet-hair rollers sell for $4–$8.

**Recommended path:** start as a **content-first affiliate** in one niche. Post demo videos, earn commission through Amazon Associates now and TikTok Shop Affiliate once eligible. Open our own store only after a product shows real purchase intent and a supplier quote confirms the margin. That's the closest thing to $0 that's real. See [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md).

**Niche:** home cleaning / "satisfying before-and-after." It's demo-driven, evergreen, filmable at home with hands only, and carries low IP and safety-claim risk.

## Margin model (planning only)

Contribution per order **before any ad spend**:

price − (unit + shipping + duty) − card fees (2.9% + $0.30) − 5% returns allowance.

| Configuration | Landed cost | Contribution per order |
|---|---|---|
| Pressure washer gun @ $69.99 | $35.65–$51.49 | $12.67–$28.51 (18–41%) |
| Pressure washer gun @ $49.99 | $35.65–$51.49 | −$5.75–$10.09 |
| Spin scrubber @ $39.99 | $17.82–$33.00 | $3.53–$18.71 |
| Spin scrubber @ $29.99 | $17.82–$33.00 | −$5.68–$9.50 |
| Pet hair roller ×2 @ $19.99 | $7.72–$24.90 | −$6.79–$10.39 |
| Pet hair roller ×1 @ $14.99 | $6.36–$16.95 | −$3.45–$7.14 |

**Assumptions:**
- Shipping: $5–9 for light items and $8–12 for a scrubber. The pressure washer gun is assumed at $12–20 because it carries a battery and weighs roughly 1.5–2.5 kg, which is unverified.
- The only dated CJPacket test I found was **$12.07 for ~500 g** (Oct 2026), so these shipping figures may be optimistic. ([Spocket review](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/cjdropshipping-overview), [CJ fee page](https://cjdropshipping.com/service-fee))
- The low end of every range assumes the cheapest listing and the lowest duty. Ranges this wide mean **we don't know the margin until we have a real quote.**

## Decision cards

### 1. Cordless pressure washer gun (21V kit, 2 batteries + foam cannon)

**Disposition:** **Advance to one named check:** a US-warehouse supplier quote.

- **Buying reason:** cleaning a car, patio or fence without a hose-and-outlet setup. Giftable to homeowners and car owners.
- **Evidence:**
  - Tracker pages show individual TikTok listings selling thousands of units after viral videos. ([EchoTik](https://www.echotik.live/products/1731236289885213002))
  - The Amazon cordless-washer top-10 average price is about $108, and the leading listing has an estimated 10k sales per month (July 2026 snapshot). ([ASINSight](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/cordless-pressure-washer))
  - Budget 21V kits sell for $44.99–$79.99 at Woot and Amazon. ([Woot](https://tools.woot.com/offers/21v-cordless-pressure-washer-w-case-2), [Power Tools Insider](https://www.powertoolsinsider.com/?p=3055))
- **Supply:** AliExpress listings show 21V kits at $18.92–$22.49 per unit. ([AliExpress search](https://www.aliexpress.us/w/wholesale-21v-car-washer.html), [item](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256811636875830.html)) Some listings exclude the battery. Identity is **visual neighbour only**: battery Ah, PSI and what's in the box are unverified.
- **Demo:** excellent, and very repeatable. Dirty driveway → clean stripe. Car wheel → foam → rinse.
- **Strongest contrary evidence:**
  - The lithium battery limits shipping routes. ([USPS rules summary](https://parcelpath.com/shipping-companies/usps/usps-shipping-restrictions/))
  - The item is heavy.
  - Amazon competitors sit at $45–$80.
  - At $49.99 the model can lose money.
- **Revisit condition:** a supplier quote that is delivered to the US (duty included), ships from a US warehouse in 7 days or less, and leaves contribution of $20 or more at $59.99–$69.99.

### 2. Electric spin scrubber (cordless, multi-head)

**Disposition:** **Rejected for our own store** at current evidence. **Pursue as affiliate content.**

- **Buying reason:** scrubbing tub, tile and grout without kneeling or arm strain.
- **Evidence:**
  - Amazon's top spin scrubber is priced at $39.99 with about 12.7k reviews and an estimated 10k sales per month. The top-10 average price is $28.58 (April 2026). ([ASINSight](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/spin-scrubber))
  - TikTok Shop listings show 48k–57k cumulative units sold. ([TikTok Shop listing](https://www.tiktokshop.com/pdp/2026-electric-spin-scrubber-ipx7-waterproof-multi-surface/1731957964105749423))
- **Supply:** AliExpress listings run from about $7 to $32. Quality and specs vary widely. ([AliExpress](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-electric-spin-scrubber.html))
- **Contrary:** deal sites regularly show $15–$23 on Amazon. ([Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/19375419-puftem-cordless-handheld-electric-spin-scrubber-w-6-brush-heads-14-95)) Our store would have to charge more than Amazon for a slower delivery.
- **Why keep it:** it's the ideal demo product for the cleaning niche, and as an affiliate we don't carry the landed cost.

### 3. Reusable pet hair remover roller

**Disposition:** **Rejected for our own store.** **Pursue as affiliate content.**

- **Buying reason:** pet owners fighting fur on couches and bedding, with no refills to buy.
- **Evidence:** the branded original (ChomChom) went viral on TikTok and has a very large Amazon review count. ([Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/smart-shopping/chomchom-pet-hair-remover), [SheKnows](https://www.sheknows.com/living/articles/2539652/chom-chom-pet-hair-remover/))
- **Supply:** CJ lists generic rollers at $1.39–$5.68 and AliExpress at about $1.09–$4.93. ([CJ listing](https://cjdropshipping.com/product/2-in-1-pet-hair-removal-roller-multifunctional-portable-washable-hair-removal-brush-pet-supplies-p-1777181071063396352.html), [AliExpress](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256806474158858.html))
- **Contrary:**
  - Walmart generics sell for $3.61–$7.99, and ChomChom itself for $15.99. ([Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Reusable-Lint-Roller-Sticky-Life-Multi-Purpose-Pet-Hair-Dust-Lint-Remover-Clothing-Upholstery-Cars-Sustainable-Washable-Efficient/5828797933))
  - Shipping alone eats the margin on a single unit.
  - There's a reported (unverified) history of patent action against lookalikes.

### 4. Squishy / mochi toys and squishy advent calendars

**Disposition:** **Rejected for this operator.**

- **Evidence:** this is the top TikTok Shop toy category right now. ([FastMoss](https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/best-selling-toys-on-tiktok-shop-what-sold-last-christmas-and-whats-trending-for-2026/))
- **Why rejected:**
  - Kids' products need a Children's Product Certificate backed by third-party lab testing, and since July 2026 that certificate must be e-filed at customs entry. ([CPSC](https://www.cpsc.gov/Business--Manufacturing/Testing-Certification/Childrens-Product-Certificate/))
  - In August 2026 the CPSC and US customs blocked about 356k violative squishy toys at the ports. ([news](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/08/13/authorities-warn-fake-squishy-toys-could-pose-serious-risks-children-tips-keep-your-kids-safe/))
  - Too much compliance and seizure risk for a beginner dropshipper.

### 5. Migraine ice caps, hair-growth serum combs and similar items

**Disposition:** **Rejected.** Their selling angle depends on health claims we can't substantiate for a generic product.

## What would change these decisions

- A real CJ or US-warehouse quote (requires a free CJ account, which only you can create). This would turn the margin ranges into single numbers.
- Comment-level purchase intent ("where do I get this") on our own videos for a specific product.
- A differentiated offer that Amazon can't easily match, such as a bundle, a kit or a niche-specific version.
